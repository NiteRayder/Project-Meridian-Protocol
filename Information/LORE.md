# Project: Meridian Protocol — Story Overview
## Overview
To the public the world is ordinary. Beneath the surface, the Organization for the Study of Anomalous Entities (OSAE) investigates, contains, and studies phenomena that contradict normal explanation. MERIDIAN is OSAE’s research project that catalogues and experiments on inconsistencies in historical records, memories, and physical evidence.
Core Premise
Researchers on MERIDIAN discover that reality itself can branch and shift: the same people, places, and events can diverge across overlapping states. Some branches are minor—different notes in a log—while others are catastrophic, with large-scale loss and altered physical evidence. The project’s work reveals patterns in how these branches appear and how certain anomalous phenomena interact with them.
### Key Figures:
* Maxwell McArthur Burwell — A curious 16-year-old (20 in one later meeting) and the game’s primary protagonist. Raised apart from OSAE’s anomalous operations, Maxwell is driven to uncover what his parents hid. He becomes central to MERIDIAN’s studies when AE-DC-01 captures him and exposes him to multiple realities.
* Arthur and Laura Burwell — Maxwell’s parents and senior OSAE agents. Their secrecy is meant to protect Maxwell but also conceals dangerous truths about MERIDIAN. Their roles appear differently across branches (alive, deceased, or altered by anomalies).
* Dr. Jayce Ray Vance — Director of Project MERIDIAN; an authoritative scientist whose research pushes the project into morally ambiguous territory.
### Principal Threats and Motifs:
A* E-DC-01 ("the Entity") — An anomalous phenomenon that manifests through an image (the Entity.jpeg). Exposure produces cognitive, digital, and environmental disruptions. AE-DC-01 can distort perceptions and interact with the underlying branching structure of reality.
* Hunters and Blood Motif — Some escaped anomalies track scent cues tied to blood. Even a single stylized drop in the environment can signal danger and change enemy behavior.
* The Hand-Cut Clue — Small, consistent environmental details (a cut hand or a single blood drop) are used as player cues to reveal branching differences and to foreshadow danger.
### Narrative Anchor: the 2035 Clip
A brief prologue cinematic shows a possible 2035: a ruined city and Maxwell kneeling in grief, screaming, "WHY DID YOU TAKE THEM FROM ME?" The clip functions as a worst-case anchor—an outcome the player may avoid or confront as the story unfolds.
This clip is not a fixed future but a potential reality that can be influenced by player choices and discoveries throughout the game. It can also be changed or avoided entirely depending on the player's actions, serving as a constant reminder of the stakes involved in the branching narrative. Therefor, depending on the previous run, it can be completely different, showing the consequences of the player's choices and the fragility of reality within the game world.
Gameplay & Story Structure
The game is a story-driven survival and exploration experience. Players move through OSAE facilities, solve camera/lock puzzles, collect records, and manage risk when branches diverge. At times NPCs become playable (and Maxwell may become an NPC) depending on the reality branch. The core loop emphasizes investigation, environmental storytelling, and resourceful avoidance of anomalies.
The mechanics of branching realities are central to both the narrative and gameplay. Players’ choices and discoveries can lead to different branches, affecting the story, character fates, and environmental conditions. The game encourages replayability, as players can explore multiple outcomes and uncover hidden truths about the anomalies and OSAE’s operations.
Some of the mechanics act like ROBLOX DOORS, where players must navigate through different states of reality to progress. The game also incorporates elements of psychological horror, with tension and suspense building as players uncover the consequences of their actions across branches. Players may encounter multiple versions of the same reality.
The game can alter based on a small decision or discovery, creating a sense of fragility and consequence. Players may find themselves in a familiar location that has changed drastically due to their previous actions or the influence of anomalies.
It is designed to be a narrative-driven experience, with a focus on storytelling, character development, and the exploration of complex themes such as memory, identity, and the nature of reality. The branching narrative structure allows for a rich and immersive experience, where players can uncover different facets of the story through their choices and interactions with the game world.
This game also has open-world elements, allowing players to explore the OSAE facilities and surrounding environments at their own pace. The open-world design encourages exploration and discovery, with hidden areas, secret documents, and environmental storytelling that reward players for their curiosity and attention to detail.
Certain areas may be locked or inaccessible until players have made specific choices or discovered certain items, creating a sense of progression and accomplishment as they unlock new parts of the world. There are also side missions as well as main story missions, allowing players to engage with the narrative in different ways and providing additional context and depth to the overall story.
There are also secret endings that can only be unlocked once players have completed specific objectives or made certain choices throughout the game. They are also only accessinle after the player has completed the main story, providing a sense of closure and reward for their efforts. There are also collectebles that can be found throughout the game, which provide additional lore and backstory for players who are interested in delving deeper into the world of Project: Meridian Protocol.
