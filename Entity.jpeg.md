# The Take-Over
It was peaceful that night. It all happened so fast. Everything was so... wrong.

It was late, and I was talking with my friends through discord. Then one of them sends me an image. It's been floating on the internet for some time, but I'd never looked at it. The rumors around it gave me the worst feelings of being watched.

But that night was different.

I'd decided to stop being chickenshit and get over it. It's just an image after all. Right...? How much damage could that do? Lord, I was wrong for that assumption. And I was in morbid luck that night, because I didn't have to look it up – it was already in my DM's.

So, I opened the DM and looked at the image and made up my mind that the rumors were made by terrified twelve- and fourteen-year old's. This was obviously just a .jpeg piece of art. That was until the feeling of being watched started.

I panicked. It all felt wrong. My body felt numb, but I was overly aware at the same time. Then out of nowhere, my discord sent a ping @Maxiwell:

Maxiwell:

```powershell
PS C:\Users\Discord> I know where you live, Maxwell. I can see you through the screen.
```

"What the fuck...? How- "

Then my mouse cursor started moving on its own. It went to the PowerShell and typed:

```powershell
PS C:\Users\Victim> Run-Path "556\\ISEEYOU.zEntity.exe"
```

What the fuck is this shit? I thought in terror. Someone had to be playing a prank on me. It must be Roland. Bro jailbroke a Linux laptop at the age of 7. I wouldn't put-

Terminal output:

```powershell
Not Roland, mwahaha. This is you; you are me.
```

I wanted to get up, to shut the computer down. And the funny thing is I tried to, but the power button would not work. Then I got this overwhelming feeling of being out of control. Like my body wasn't mine, or at least the movements weren't. I knew what I was doing, but I wasn't the one who was doing it. It was terrifying.

Entity:

```powershell
> Don't look away. Give in to it... take that feeling and follow it.
```

No. I can't, I've got too much still to do... I've read smile dog and know exactly how this shit goes. Wait, how does a smile dog go again? Shit... it's already doing a system take over. But not of my computer... of my brain. It was taking my brain and resetting it, making me just a mindless host corpse so that it could have a physical manifestation. It won't be long before the total takeover is successful. BUT I don't give in... I turn my VPN on instead. And that PISSED IT OFF.

Entity:

```powershell
> WHY CAN'T WE SEE YOOIYYTTUTYUOTOUTOTUOOUYOU?

> TURN IT OFF!!!

> LET US IN!!!
```
The world around me starts to warp. The reality of everything starts to feel wrong. The shadows bend in impossible ways. The corners are slightly more or slightly less than their default 90 degrees. The ceiling is slanted but straight at the same time. The air becomes thick and hard to breathe. And suddenly my mind goes blank. All thought cease to exist. And, the take over is complete. I have no fight left... and so I give in.

# The Rescue
I am late. Maxwell and I were having dinner tonight. At his place. I swear that he said that it was going to be 5pm last week, but I guess he said 4pm. So, I should have left at two, but I left at three instead. Now I am running late.

When I finally arrive, the energy suffocates me. It feels... wrong. I cant breathe, my skin crawls, and the hair on my neck stands on end. But I ignore it. I walk up to his 204 apartment door, and go to knock, but it opens as my hand lands on the door. That is not good, that is not normal. I let myself in at this point because I am now highly concerned. And then every thing is spinning and not at the same time. I am drawn to the kitchen for some reason, and then I found out why. Because Maxwell is glued to the screen of his laptop, but it's not right. He's brainless, not moving – not even to greet me.

Then I hear his voice, but it seems to reverberate from all over the house.

"Alex. Leave. Run. RUN, OR IT WILL GET YOU TOO!" then in the same breath, "Stay. We had plans tonight, remember? I have made dinner for you! It's already at the table."

By this time, I have deducted what happened. I tried to tell him multiple times that that image wasn't just a normal creepy pasta, that it was real. We have many residential patients because of the mental damage it causes. The only way to reboot them was the highly illegal and torturous shock therapy. Then we have special forces thoroughly sweep their residence with emp grenades to kill the system. After that you must destroy the hard drives and motherboards of all phones, laptops, desktop PCs, tablets, TVs; anything that the system can attach to.

I hear the TV suddenly turn on, and it's the image. I know it is without looking at it, because of the feeling of being watched.

"Alex, don't give in."

I turn to the computer and stab the screen with my Swiss-army knife. I must put that down in order to drag Maxwell away from it. The screech that reverberates throughout the apartment complex is agonizing, and then I feel my ear oozing. I put my hand to it, and sure enough, it's bleeding. The terminal appears on the TV.
